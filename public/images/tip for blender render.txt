# Digital invitation → smooth on phone (recipe)

Project: `Downloads\blender\blender\project\Untitled.blend` (scene "Invitation", EEVEE, 1080x1560, 30fps, 300 frames)

## Why v2 lagged
- 2 full-screen videos at once (background + transparent flower overlay)
- Overlay was big: 1440x1040, CRF 18 → 10.7 MB for 10 s; phone redrew it on top 30x/sec via WebGL
- Too many flowers = lots of fine moving detail = high bitrate

## What fixed it
1. **Less detail in Blender**: hid ~60% of flowers/vines/grass (hide, not delete), moved top flowers up (+1.3) and bottom flowers down (−1.2) with delta_location, open middle for text, butterflies 65% (delta_scale).
2. **One opaque video when possible (v3)**: render "Full Preview" layer once → `invitation.mp4` 1.2 MB. No alpha, no canvas = hardware decoder does everything.
3. **Overlay only for small things (v4)**: background video + butterflies-only overlay (mostly empty → 0.6 MB, 540x780). Butterfly shadows stay in background (butterflies `visible_camera=False` in bg pass).

## Settings to reuse
- Render: 1080 wide PNG frames (downscaling gives cleaner edges), EEVEE
- Web video: **H.264 MP4, 720x1040, 30 fps, yuv420p, CRF 22–23, preset slow, -g 30, +faststart, no audio**
- Overlay (transparent): colour LEFT + alpha mask RIGHT in one MP4 (hstack), drawn with WebGL; keep it small/low-res
- Poster: first frame JPG, shown instantly
- Don't: 60fps, 1080p for web, CRF 18, GIF/WebP animation, full-screen transparent layers

## HTML rules
- `<video autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="auto" poster="poster.jpg">`
- `position: fixed; object-fit: cover` for background
- Overlay: draw only on new video frame (`requestVideoFrameCallback`), pause on `visibilitychange`, `pointer-events:none`
- Keep overlay video in sync with background (nudge `currentTime` if drift > 0.12 s)

## Steps next time
1. Design in Blender, keep middle empty for text, don't overcrowd
2. Decide: one video (simplest) or bg + small overlay
3. Double-click `make_web_v3.bat` (one video) or `make_web_v4.bat` (bg + butterflies)
   - delete old frame folders first (`full_v3` / `bg_v4`, `fly_v4`) after changing the .blend
4. Upload everything in `web_v3` or `web_v4`; test on a real phone

Note: on tall phones `object-fit: cover` crops ~15% of each side — keep important things away from the side edges (or render taller).
